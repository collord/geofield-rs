geofield web harness — third-party and component licences
==========================================================

This web application loads TWO separately-compiled WebAssembly modules. They are
distinct works, each governed by its own licence, combined only at load time by
the host page (the browser instantiates them as two WebAssembly instances and
wires the engine's imports to the kernel's exports at runtime).

  geofield_kernel.wasm  — EUPL-1.2
      The universal-cokriging scalar-field kernel (re-derived from GemPy's
      interpolation math). Governed by the European Union Public Licence v. 1.2.
      Source: crates/geofield-kernel. Full licence: that crate's LICENSE file.

  geofield_bg.wasm      — BSD-3-Clause
      The meshing/contouring engine and its WebAssembly bindings. Independent
      work; it does NOT embed the EUPL kernel — it reaches the kernel's field
      oracle across a WebAssembly module boundary (runtime imports), so this
      artifact contains no EUPL-covered code.
      Source: crates/geofield (+ crates/geofield-types, BSD-3-Clause).

Why this matters: the EUPL's reciprocity attaches to a Derivative Work / a fused
binary. Keeping the kernel in its own artifact and importing it at runtime means
geofield_bg.wasm is not a fused binary of EUPL code, and remains BSD-3-Clause; the
EUPL obligations travel only with geofield_kernel.wasm.

The bundled JavaScript also includes third-party libraries (React, three.js,
wasm-bindgen glue) under their respective permissive licences.
